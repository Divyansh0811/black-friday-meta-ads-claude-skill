# Compliance check (US + Meta)

Not legal advice. Flag anything uncertain for the user to confirm.

## Prices and discounts
- [ ] **"Was" / strike-through price is genuine**: the product was actually offered at that price for a reasonably substantial, recent period (FTC Guides, 16 CFR 233). California expects at least the past 3 months. A "sale" that runs all season makes the "was" price fictional.
- [ ] **"Up to X%"**: at least 10% of the advertised items are at the maximum discount (BBB Code / NAD). Safer: put the hero product's real discount in the headline instead.
- [ ] The offer, numbers and deadline are **identical** in the ad, landing page, promo bar, code, email and SMS.

## Urgency and scarcity
- [ ] No countdown timers that reset. Deadlines are real, and the ad's scheduled end date matches the deadline in the creative.
- [ ] Stock claims ("almost gone", "sold out last year") come from real inventory or real history.
- [ ] Email/SMS subject lines are true. "Ends tonight" followed by an extension has triggered lawsuits (Washington's CEMA). Only extend what you announced honestly.

## Meta ad policies
- [ ] No deceptive pricing or misleading claims.
- [ ] Health and beauty: no before/after transformation images for cosmetic procedures or weight loss; claims must be supportable; don't target under-18s for restricted products.
- [ ] Every claim in the kit comes from the brand's site (brand_profile) or the user.

## Allow time for ad review
Meta reviews most ads within ~24 hours, but Q4 can be slower: submit video ~72 hours before it must go live, and schedule start/end dates instead of editing live ads.
