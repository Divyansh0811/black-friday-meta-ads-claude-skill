# Launch plan and phases (BFCM 2026)

| Phase | Dates (2026) | Creative needed | Launch by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finish + warm-up | now to ~18 Nov | Teasers, VIP/waitlist lead capture, evergreen UGC; submit everything for review | now |
| VIP / early access | ~19-25 Nov | Early-access statics, stacked offer, founder video, gift guide | ~18 Nov |
| Black Friday | Thu 26 - Fri 27 Nov | "Black Friday is live" offer statics, price math, UGC, demo/unboxing | scheduled for 26 Nov |
| Weekend | Sat 28 - Sun 29 Nov | Doorbusters / daily deals; keep spend steady (efficiency often rebounds Saturday) | 27 Nov |
| Cyber Monday | Mon 30 Nov | NEW creative with a new hook, not a re-edit of BF ads; "cart closes at midnight" | 29 Nov |
| Gifting | 1-17 Dec | Gift guides, price bands, shipping cutoffs (USPS Ground 17 Dec) | 30 Nov |

## Structure and testing (short window)
- Keep it simple: few campaigns, broad targeting, Advantage+ sales campaigns. Run sale ads **alongside** evergreen ads; don't switch evergreens off.
- **Pre-test now** (before 19 Nov): use Meta's Creative Testing tool (2-5 ads, ~20% of budget or less) to test hooks and offer framing on early-access traffic; move the winners into BF.
- At peak: **duplicate, don't edit** (big edits reset learning). Watch hourly; if results beat target, the budget is probably too low.
- 3-5 creatives per ad set; replace when frequency climbs or Meta flags creative fatigue.

## What to measure
Hook rate (3-second views / impressions), CTR, conversion rate and cost per purchase by concept; frequency; blended MER and new-customer cost, not only platform ROAS.

## Retargeting vs prospecting
- Prospecting: proof and demo, plus the offer.
- Retargeting (cart abandoners, viewers, past buyers): pure offer statics, catalog ads with strike-through, "ends Monday".
