# Formats and specs (Meta, 2026)

## Must-have formats for BFCM
1. **Offer-first static**: the number is the hero. ("25% OFF" + product + deadline)
2. **Price-math static**: strike-through price, new price, "you save $X".
3. **Stacked-offer static**: offer + gift + free shipping, as a short list.
4. **Gift guide**: products by price band ("Gifts under $50").
5. **Creator UGC video**: offer on screen by second 3; real use, real face.
6. **Founder video**: "We rarely discount. Here's why this week is different." (only if true)
7. **Unboxing / demo video** with the offer as an end card.
8. **Text-only contrarian static**: e.g. "No 'biggest sale ever.' Just 25% off the 3 things you need."
9. **Catalog / Advantage+ catalog ads** with dynamic overlays (strike-through, % off, free shipping). These need a valid `sale_price` and sale dates in the product feed; the label disappears when the sale window ends.
10. **"Wrapper" of a proven winner**: the same winning ad, duplicated, with a BF offer overlay or end card.

Meta's guidance: use at least 3 asset types (static, carousel, Reels), and 9:16 video with sound.

## Variety beats volume of look-alikes
Meta groups ads that look alike and treats them as one. Vary the story, the persona (gift buyer vs self buyer vs VIP) and the visual hook, not just the headline. Plan 3-5 creatives per ad set and refresh as frequency climbs.

## Sizes and safe zones
| Placement | Size | Keep text/offer/logo out of |
|---|---|---|
| Feed | 4:5 at 1440×1800 | Edges; centre key content so a 1:1 crop still works |
| Reels / Stories | 9:16 at 1440×2560 | Top 14% (~358px), bottom 35% (~896px), 6% each side (~86px) |

## Copy lengths
- **Primary text**: Meta suggests 50-150 characters; the first ~125 show before "See more". Write a short (≤125) and a long version.
- **Headline**: ~27 characters shows fully; up to ~40 is fine.
- **Reels primary text**: ~44 characters.

## Video rules
- The offer and the message land in the first 3 seconds.
- Burned-in captions; works with sound off and sound on.
- If the creative already carries offer text, turn off Advantage+ automatic text overlays.

## Copy formulas
- Line 1: **[specific offer] + [hero product] + [real end date]**. "Black Friday" is a label, not the hook.
- Headline: **[offer number] + [object]** ("$60 off the Dutch Oven") or "25% off sitewide, ends Mon".
- Hooks to rotate: contrarian ("Don't buy X this Black Friday unless…"), price math, "we rarely discount", gift problem ("What to get the person who…"), creator nearly missing the deal, social proof, Cyber Monday "cart closes at midnight".
- Avoid: "biggest sale ever" with no number, vague "up to X%", leading with "SALE", black-and-red when the brand's own colours stand out more, and an offer that differs from the landing page.
- Write separate concepts for **gift buyers** and **self buyers** (many shoppers buy for themselves in BFCM).
