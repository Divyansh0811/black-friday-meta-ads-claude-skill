# Offer playbook (BFCM)

Sources are mostly agencies and vendors (Common Thread Collective, Foxwell Digital, AdManage), so treat numbers as directional.

## Choosing the mechanic
| Goal / situation | Lead with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| More orders (volume) | % off sitewide or on the hero product | Discounts drive conversion volume |
| Bigger orders (AOV) | Gift with purchase over $X, tiered "spend $X save $Y", bundles/sets | Raises AOV with less margin pain (a gift worth $120 that costs $20) |
| Brand that rarely/never discounts | Early access, gift, free shipping, a set, or a "why we don't discount" founder video | Protects price; the contrarian angle stands out |
| Strong winners already | Keep the core offer stable all season; change how it is framed | Consistency builds trust (Foxwell) |

## Rule of 100 (Jonah Berger)
- Price **under $100** → say it as **%** ("25% off").
- Price **over $100** → say it as **$** ("$60 off").
- Bundles or spend thresholds → say the **total $ saved**.

## Depth benchmarks
- In 2024 most BFCM offers sat at 20-25%; about 30% of brands went 30%+ (CTC).
- Perceived value beat the nominal number in 2025: a stacked or clearly-valued offer can beat a bigger flat %.
- If competitors (from Reckon's `offers`) cluster at one depth, either match it with clearer value, or step outside it with a different mechanic (gift, bundle, $ off).

## The offer ladder (by phase)
Each step distinct and a little more attractive, never fake:
1. **Pre-sale / VIP**: early access for subscribers (often + an extra ~10%). Lead capture.
2. **Black Friday**: the main offer, live. "Black Friday is live."
3. **Weekend**: doorbusters / daily deals keep it fresh.
4. **Cyber Monday**: a new reason (a bigger tier, a new gift, a new product) and NEW creative.
5. **Gifting (to ~17 Dec)**: gift guides, price bands, shipping-cutoff messages. Extend only if planned and announced honestly.
Keep a stronger "parachute" offer ready in case results fall short.

## Showing the offer in creative
- The offer is the biggest thing on screen, readable in about 1 second, in the top third.
- Real strike-through price + "$ saved" (only with a genuine former price; see compliance.md).
- One line of proof (rating, review, award, "bestseller").
- A real deadline ("ends Mon 30 Nov").
- Hero product named: "$60 off the Dutch Oven" beats "Up to 50% off everything".
